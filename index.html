About Us

Care led by consultants, held by a calm environment.

The Wellbeing House was founded on a simple belief: that mental health care in Pakistan deserves the same privacy, dignity, and clinical rigor as any other branch of medicine. Set within a quiet residential address in Bani Gala, we treat patients from Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and across the country.

Our approach pairs consultant-led psychiatric treatment with structured therapy, so that medication, if needed, is never the only plan.

Founder

Dr. Shahid Ali Khan

Founder, Director & Consultant Psychiatrist, The Wellbeing House.

Dr. Shahid Ali Khan founded The Wellbeing House to provide a facility that was, until now, hard to find in Pakistan: psychiatric care built entirely around ethical practice, clinical honesty, and patient dignity. It is a standard he has carried across his career and built this institute specifically to uphold.

He holds an M.B.B.S. and F.C.P.S. in Psychiatry, with 20 years of experience in the field. He previously served as Head of Department (Psychiatry) at NESCOM Hospital, Islamabad, and as Consultant Psychiatrist at Maroof International Hospital — experience that directly shaped the ethical, standards-driven approach this institute was built on. Today, alongside The Wellbeing House, he also practices at Greenfield Hospital and at the Islamabad Specialist Clinic (F-8 Markaz).

His goal for The Wellbeing House is simple: the most comfortable, judgement-free environment for anyone facing a mental health issue or disorder, delivered to the same standard as any other branch of medicine.

"There is no health without mental health."

Consultant Psychologist

Ms. Marrium Mir

Ms. Marrium Mir is a Consultant Psychologist and Psychotherapist at The Wellbeing House, and a gold medalist in her Master's degree, leading therapy and counselling alongside the psychiatric team to build a complete, ongoing care plan for every patient. She also serves as the visiting consultant psychologist at Temar Medical & Cardiac Hospital.

She previously worked at Benazir Bhutto Hospital (BBH) for two years, and is a member of the American Psychological Association (APA) and the International Society of Substance Use Prevention and Treatment Professionals (ISSUP).

Treatment Policy

Clear terms, so care stays the only focus.

How confidentiality, appointments, admissions, and fees work at The Wellbeing House.

Confidentiality

All consultations, records, and diagnoses are kept strictly confidential and are never shared without your written consent.

Appointments & Cancellations

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel a booked appointment, so the slot can be offered to another patient.

Admission Process

Inpatient admission follows an initial psychiatric evaluation, so that every stay begins with a clear, agreed treatment plan.

Family Involvement

Family members are involved in care planning and visitation with the patient's consent, coordinated directly with the clinical team.

Fees & Payment

Consultation and treatment fees are shared in advance of every appointment, with no hidden charges added later.

Emergency Situations

We are equipped to manage psychiatric emergencies on-site. For medical emergencies, please contact local emergency services or your nearest hospital right away.
